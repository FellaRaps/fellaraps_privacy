Privacy Policy for FellaRaps

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Overview

FellaRaps is a music recording and sharing app. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

Data We Collect

  • Username — a display name you choose when you first use the app
  • Audio recordings — tracks you choose to post to the Fellas feed

How Your Data Is Used

Your username and posted tracks are stored in Apple's CloudKit and displayed publicly in the Fellas feed to other users of the app. Tracks you do not post remain stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded.

Push Notifications

FellaRaps may send push notifications when other users post new tracks. You can disable these at any time in your device's Settings.

Data Deletion

You can remove any posted track from the Fellas feed at any time by deleting it within the app. This permanently removes it from our servers.

Third Parties

FellaRaps does not share your data with any third parties. Audio and user data are stored exclusively through Apple CloudKit.
